How it works

Three steps from call to settlement.

No accounts, no API keys to rotate, no monthly bill. Just a wallet and a price per call.

1
Fund or pay direct
Deposit credit once, or pay the exact price with every call.
2
Call the service
The contract meters each call against the provider’s daily cap.
3
Instant settlement
The provider is credited the moment the call lands, receipt on-chain.
For providers
List a service, get paid per call.

Set a price, an optional daily cap per caller, and register on-chain. Earnings accrue as you’re called — withdraw your full balance whenever you want, in one transaction.

  • Flat 2.5% platform fee, split off every call
  • Nothing pushed to your wallet automatically
  • Withdraw your earnings anytime
For callers & agents
Pay only for what you use.

Deposit prepaid credit once and skip the per-call payment step, or send the exact price with every request — no setup, better for occasional callers.

  • Prepaid credit for agents making many calls in a row
  • Pay-per-call, one shot, no account needed
  • Every call settles with a receipt on-chain
